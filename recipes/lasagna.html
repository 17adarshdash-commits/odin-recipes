Lasagna

Description

To make a classic homemade lasagna, start by simmering a rich meat sauce using ground beef, Italian sausage, crushed tomatoes, onions, garlic, and dried oregano. While the sauce thickens, boil your lasagna noodles until just al dente and whip up a cheese filling by combining creamy ricotta, shredded mozzarella, grated Parmesan, an egg, and fresh parsley. Assemble the dish in a 9x13 inch pan by spreading a thin layer of sauce on the bottom, then alternating layers of overlapping noodles, the ricotta mixture, and more meat sauce, repeating until the pan is full and finishing with a heavy sprinkle of mozzarella on top. Bake covered with foil at **375°F** (190°C) for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 25 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbling, making sure to let it rest for at least 15 minutes before slicing so the layers set perfectly.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles: 12 to 16 ounces (standard boil-and-drain noodles or oven-ready sheets).
  • Meat: 1 pound ground beef (many prefer a 50/50 mix of ground beef and sweet Italian sausage).
  • Aromatics: 1 medium yellow onion (diced) and 2 to 3 cloves of garlic (minced).
  • Tomatoes: 1 can (28 ounces) of crushed tomatoes and 1 can (6 ounces) of tomato paste.
  • Seasoning: 1 tablespoon olive oil (for sautéing), 1 to 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning (or equal parts dried oregano and basil), 1 teaspoon salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper.
  • Ricotta: 15 to 16 ounces of whole-milk ricotta cheese.
  • Mozzarella: 3 to 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (divided: half for the filling, half for the melted topping).
  • Parmesan: ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese.
  • Binder & Flavor: 1 large egg (lightly beaten to bind the cheese so it doesn't run when sliced) and 2 tablespoons of fresh chopped parsley.

Steps

  1. Prep the Noodles and Oven: Skip boiling if using oven-ready sheets.Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the lasagna noodles until al dente (usually 1 to 2 minutes less than the package instructions). Drain and lay them flat on parchment paper or foil so they do not stick together.
  2. Simmer the Meat Sauce: Takes about 15 to 20 minutes.In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the ground meat. Drain any excess fat, then add the diced onions and minced garlic, sautéing until soft. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, olive oil, and seasonings. Reduce the heat to low and let it simmer so the flavors meld and the sauce thickens.
  3. Mix the Cheese Filling: In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, the egg, half of the shredded mozzarella, the grated Parmesan, and the fresh parsley. Stir until completely smooth and incorporated. The egg acts as a binder to keep the cheese from running out when you eventually cut the lasagna.
  4. Assemble the Layers:Always start with sauce on the bottom of the pan.In a 9x13 inch baking dish, spread about a cup of the meat sauce evenly across the bottom (this prevents the noodles from sticking and burning). Then, layer in this order:Noodles: 3 to 4 slightly overlapping strips.Cheese: Spread a third of the ricotta mixture.Sauce: Spoon a layer of meat sauce over the cheese.Repeat this sequence two more times. Finish with a final layer of noodles, any remaining meat sauce, and an even sprinkle of the reserved mozzarella cheese on top.
  5. Bake:Cover with foil to trap steam and prevent drying out.Tent the baking dish with aluminum foil (spray the underside of the foil with non-stick spray or tent it high enough so it doesn't stick to the cheese). Bake for 25 minutes. Carefully remove the foil and bake for another 20 to 25 minutes, or until the cheese is melted, slightly golden, and the edges are bubbling.
  6. Rest Before Slicing: Do not slice immediately.Remove the lasagna from the oven and let it stand at room temperature for at least 15 to 20 minutes. This allows the pasta to absorb excess moisture and the melted cheese to set, ensuring your slices hold their neat, layered shape when served.

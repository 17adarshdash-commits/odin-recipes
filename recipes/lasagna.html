Lasagna

Description

To make a classic homemade lasagna, start by simmering a rich meat sauce using ground beef, Italian sausage, crushed tomatoes, onions, garlic, and dried oregano. While the sauce thickens, boil your lasagna noodles until just al dente and whip up a cheese filling by combining creamy ricotta, shredded mozzarella, grated Parmesan, an egg, and fresh parsley. Assemble the dish in a 9x13 inch pan by spreading a thin layer of sauce on the bottom, then alternating layers of overlapping noodles, the ricotta mixture, and more meat sauce, repeating until the pan is full and finishing with a heavy sprinkle of mozzarella on top. Bake covered with foil at **375°F** (190°C) for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 25 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbling, making sure to let it rest for at least 15 minutes before slicing so the layers set perfectly.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles: 12 to 16 ounces (standard boil-and-drain noodles or oven-ready sheets).
  • Meat: 1 pound ground beef (many prefer a 50/50 mix of ground beef and sweet Italian sausage).
  • Aromatics: 1 medium yellow onion (diced) and 2 to 3 cloves of garlic (minced).
  • Tomatoes: 1 can (28 ounces) of crushed tomatoes and 1 can (6 ounces) of tomato paste.
  • Seasoning: 1 tablespoon olive oil (for sautéing), 1 to 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning (or equal parts dried oregano and basil), 1 teaspoon salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper.
  • Ricotta: 15 to 16 ounces of whole-milk ricotta cheese.
  • Mozzarella: 3 to 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (divided: half for the filling, half for the melted topping).
  • Parmesan: ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese.
  • Binder & Flavor: 1 large egg (lightly beaten to bind the cheese so it doesn't run when sliced) and 2 tablespoons of fresh chopped parsley.
